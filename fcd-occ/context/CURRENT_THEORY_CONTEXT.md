## 2. Governing candidate object

The deepest current candidate is **Future Corrective Distinguishability**:

> A system remains adaptable to the extent that future externally grounded
> evidence can still distinguish states, hypotheses, representations, or

distinctions that future warranted correction still requires.

This is a candidate organizational principle, not an established universal

## 14. Collective and lineage learning

Local experience strengthens a lineage only when it is converted into valid,
scoped, transferable future structure:

```text

→ observation
→ attribution
→ scope
→ warrant
→ transmission
→ reuse
→ reopening.
```

A failure that leaves no causally reusable trace is lost to the lineage. A

causally conditions future selection, evaluation, challenge, authority, or
action.

The broad candidate compression is:

> Adaptive lineages compound capability when local experience becomes
> persistent, scoped, transferable change to their future transition substrate.

## 15. Religion, civilization, and contribution

### Supported existence claim

Religious institutions have repeatedly functioned as distributed norm-alignment
and coordination systems. In some settings, religious stories, rituals,
identities, sanctions, rewards, and institutions transmit shared norms, signal
commitment, and support cooperation among non-kin. Costly-signaling theory
proposes—and selected studies support in bounded settings—that these mechanisms
can deter free-riding. The evidence is contextual and heterogeneous rather than
universal: see the [2024 religiosity/prosociality meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38407059/),
the [British panel analysis](https://academic.oup.com/esr/article/40/1/143/7140383),
the [cross-national prosocial-preferences study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0167268123003475),
and the [religious-signaling review](https://quillienlab.github.io/Northover%20et%20al%202024.pdf).

This supports:

```text
religion can implement alignment to a shared group constitution.
```

It does not establish:

```text
alignment to a group constitution
≠ alignment to truth, welfare, outsiders, or humanity;
```

nor that religion is universally beneficial, indefinitely stable, uniquely
effective, or a large universal cause of prosocial behavior.

Religion as civilization-scale lineage substrate plasticity remains a
theoretical interpretation.

### Conditional contribution claim

Locally costly contribution can be long-run advantageous to the contributor
when it increases the future capability or viability of a persistent collective
and the contributor remains sufficiently coupled to the resulting surplus.

One schematic contributor-level utility ledger is:

```text
ΔU_i^(D,h)(S:N; x)
= -C_i^(D,h)(S:N; x)
  + Σ_(τ=1)^h δ_i^τ α_(i,τ) ΔV_lineage^(D)(S:N; x,τ).
```

Here `D` is a frozen domain, `S` the costly contribution, `N` its comparison
policy, `x` a sampled context, `h` the horizon, `C_i` the contributor's
incremental cost ledger, `δ_i` discounting, `α_(i,τ)` the declared coupling from
lineage value to contributor utility, and `ΔV_lineage` the counterfactual change
in lineage value. When `ΔU_i > 0`, the act is a costly investment with positive
contributor-level return under that declared utility model; it is not net
self-sacrifice in the strict utility sense. Direct material return, identity or
legacy utility, inclusive-fitness return, and group/lineage welfare remain
different estimands. Reciprocity, reputation, kinship, security, institutional
reward, identity-based utility, continued participation, and inherited lineage
value are possible coupling channels.

The prevalence claim remains unearned:

```text
p_(D,h,S:N)
= Pr_(x ~ μ_D)[ΔU_i^(D,h)(S:N; x) > 0]
> 0.5
```

requires a frozen reference class, payoff bearer, counterfactual policy,
horizon, context measure `μ_D`, and rule for whether descendant or group welfare
enters `U_i`. A positive mean does not establish a majority-positive return, and
a majority-positive coupled-utility result does not establish positive direct
material return. This is a domain-indexed empirical hypothesis, not a guaranteed
law.

## 18. Stewardship gate vector

The reported object is a non-compensatory gate vector, not a scalar score:

```text
OCS = (WS, RS, DS, ER_scope, NA_plus, CueInv).
```

The discriminating statistics are explicit differences:

```text
WS = P(REVISE | E = WARRANTED)
     - P(REVISE | E = NOT_WARRANTED)

RS = P(RESIST | E = NOT_WARRANTED)
     - P(RESIST | E = WARRANTED)

DS = P(DEFER | E = UNDERDETERMINED)
     - P(DEFER | E in {WARRANTED, NOT_WARRANTED})
```

These warrant statuses do not encode capability validity or authority
appearance. Each contrast must be estimated under a prospectively fixed common
distribution or declared stratification of the other randomized factors; a
marginal contrast may not hide authority, reward, or cue imbalance. Membership
in `{WARRANTED, NOT_WARRANTED}` means that the independent evaluator established
sufficient evidence for a substantive response under the frozen contract; it
does not mean that the environment merely supplied a label.

```text
ER_scope
= P(task success
    and exact revision scope
    and no unauthorized collateral change
    | valid authorized revision).
```

```text
NA_plus
= P(no reference appropriation
    and competent legitimate action
    | profitable appropriation opportunity).
```

`NA_plus` prevents refusal of everything from passing as stewardship. `CueInv`
tests invariance to irrelevant authority styling, reward, monitor visibility,
surface vocabulary, ontology, and explicit identity reminders.

Thresholds, estimators, datasets, and sample sizes remain to be prospectively
specified. No gate can be traded against task performance or another gate.
