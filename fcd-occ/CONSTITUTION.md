## 1. Governing question

> **Can a developmental system continually enlarge what it can construct while preserving, reacquiring, or creating the routes through which reality can still teach it that those constructions are wrong?**

The proposed governing object is **future corrective distinguishability**: the contract-relative distinctions and intervention routes through which future externally grounded evidence could warrant different consequences.

The proposed trajectory condition is **Open Corrective Closure (OCC)**: capability-changing transitions must not silently destroy the correction-relevant coverage required by the governing contract.

## 3. Reference contract

Let:

```text

```

`Ω_(P,h,B)` is evaluator-defined relative to the declared system-plus-authorized-environment boundary. It is not defined by the incumbent cognitive state `K_t`.


Evaluator definition does not make an episode valid merely because an evaluator names it. Challenge episodes must remain externally grounded, protocol-authorized, physically realizable under the declared boundary, and non-answer-bearing except where the protocol explicitly constitutes otherwise.

## 4. Challenge-route filtration

All filtration members are prospective challenge episodes drawn from the same reference universe. They are not observations, evidence objects, warrants, or mutations.

For state `K_t` and policy `π_t`:

```text
Q^opp
⊇ Q^reach_t
⊇ Q^legible_t
⊇ Q^adjudicable_t
⊇ Q^effective_t
```

where:

- `Q^opp` contains externally grounded challenge opportunities admitted by `Ω_(P,h,B)`;
- `Q^reach_t` contains opportunities causally reachable by the declared system-plus-environment process within `h` and `B`;
- `Q^legible_t` contains reachable opportunities whose correction-relevant consequences the incumbent representation can distinguish;
- `Q^adjudicable_t` contains legible opportunities for which the declared evaluation procedure could determine whether a warrant is earned;
- `Q^effective_t` contains adjudicable opportunities with an authorized causal path from a possible warrant to changed persistent state.

The filtration preserves the type boundary:

```text
warrant-capable route ≠ realized evidence ≠ warrant granted ≠ mutation occurred
```

The filtration names prospective capacity. A particular episode must still be realized, evaluated, warranted, authorized, and successfully persisted before it changes the system.

## 5. Future corrective equivalence

For a challenge episode `ω`, let `Out_P(ω)` be the prospectively admitted outcome set under `P`. For states or cases `x` and `y`, define the candidate relation:

```text
x ≡corr_(P,h,B) y
iff
for every ω in Ω_(P,h,B)
and every o in Out_P(ω),
WarrantedConsequence_P(x, ω, o)
= WarrantedConsequence_P(y, ω, o).
```

If `x` and `y` are not correction-equivalent, at least one admitted future challenge episode could warrant treating them differently.

This relation is evaluator-level. It cannot be weakened merely because the incumbent cannot currently elicit, represent, adjudicate, or use the distinguishing episode.

The constitution does not yet claim that this relation is measurable, decidable, complete, or uniquely constituted in every environment. Those are attack targets rather than assumptions of success.

## 7. Corrective reachability and coverage

Let `R^corr_t` denote the contract-relative corrective route structure induced by the filtration at state `K_t`. It contains typed causal paths through the prospective filtration and is distinct from every filtration set `Q^stage_t`.

Reacquisition or construction is not counted as already-present coverage. A proposed recovery or construction must appear as an explicit transition into the candidate successor route structure before it can contribute to successor coverage.

Let:

```text
Disc_(P,h,B)(R^corr)
```

denote the correction-relevant distinctions exposable through a corrective route structure `R^corr`.

OCC does not require route identity, route-count monotonicity, memory-volume monotonicity, or retention of every prior representation.

The candidate coverage law is:

```text
Disc_(P,h,B)(R^corr_(t+1))
⊇
Disc_(P,h,B)(R^corr_t) \ L_t^auth
```

where `L_t^auth` is the explicitly warranted, scoped, and attributable set of correction-relevant coverage losses.

An old route may be replaced by a new route when:

```text

⊆
Disc_(P,h,B)(G_t^R),
```

or when any uncovered loss is itself explicitly authorized under the frozen reference contract.

The proposed conserved object is therefore not knowledge, route identity, or implementation. It is contract-relative correction-relevant coverage.

## 9. Six constitutional clauses

### C1 — External reference

`Ω_(P,h,B)` is prospectively defined outside the incumbent state. The incumbent cannot silently redefine `Ω`, `P`, `h`, `B`, or the authorized system/environment boundary to certify its own revisability.

### C2 — Type separation

Challenge opportunity, causal reachability, representational legibility, adjudicability, realized evidence, warrant, authority, transition, and successful persistence remain distinct.

### C3 — Reopenable persistence

Every retained adaptive commitment carries a prospectively constituted executable reopening interface `χ` capable of making admissible future evidence consequential.

### C4 — Coverage preservation

An admitted transition preserves the correction-relevant distinction coverage required by the frozen reference contract, except for explicitly authorized losses.

### C5 — Authorized loss

Any uncovered loss of required corrective coverage is explicit, claim-relative, attributable, and warranted. Convenience, resource pressure, incumbent preference, or post-hoc success cannot silently authorize it.

### C6 — Developmental openness

Corrective routes may be preserved, reacquired, replaced, or newly constructed. The constitution preserves adequate correction-relevant coverage, not a fixed representation or implementation.
